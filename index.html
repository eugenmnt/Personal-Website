Profile

About

Between evidence and decision: research, foresight and analysis for people who have to act on it.

I am a security and defence analyst moving into policy advice and research. My work sits between evidence and decision: I have written policy analyses for military and ministerial readers, co-authored a European Commission foresight study, and briefed EU bodies and Member States on complex findings in plain language.

My path runs through Europe’s security institutions: youth work in Sumy, Ukraine; defence research at Finabel in Brussels; technology policy at UNCTAD in Geneva; strategic foresight at the Commission’s Joint Research Centre; and analysis at the EU Satellite Centre. I believe good policy is built on evidence, open to debate and aimed at a safer, more stable Europe.

What I bring

  • Policy analysis & writingClear, decision-ready analysis for military, ministerial and EU audiences.
  • Strategic foresightHorizon scanning, Delphi panels, expert workshops, scenarios and impact assessment.
  • Evidence from open sourcesOSINT, imagery and data turned into findings that non-specialists can act on.
